# Figures and Tables

## Economic Welfare Burden of Pancreatic Cancer in 122 Low- and Middle-Income Countries, 1990–2023, with Projections to 2050

> Income elasticity = 1.0 (baseline). All monetary values in 2023 US dollars (PPP). Uncertainty intervals represent 95% UI from GBD 2023; forecast intervals represent 95% CI from ETS models.

---

## Figures

### Figure 1. Cross-sectional economic welfare burden of pancreatic cancer across 122 LMICs in 2023.

**(a)** Total value of lost welfare (VLW) attributable to pancreatic cancer in 2023, stratified by World Bank income classification. Bars represent point estimates; error bars denote 95% uncertainty intervals (UI). Upper middle-income countries (UMIC) bear the largest absolute burden (564.06 billion USD), followed by lower middle-income countries (LMIC; 80.46 billion USD) and low-income countries (LIC; 1.97 billion USD). Colour coding: red = LIC; orange = LMIC; teal = UMIC.

**(b)** Distribution of VLW as a percentage of national GDP across individual countries, stratified by income group. Box plots show median, interquartile range, and whiskers extending to 1.5 times the interquartile range. Each jittered point represents one country. Despite lower absolute VLW, some LIC and LMIC countries exhibit disproportionately high VLW/GDP ratios, indicating that the relative economic burden of pancreatic cancer can exceed that of wealthier nations.

**(c)** Sex-stratified VLW by income group. Paired bars compare male (blue) and female (red) VLW within each income stratum. Error bars denote 95% UI. Males consistently show higher VLW than females across all income groups, reflecting higher pancreatic cancer DALYs in men (male: 400.55 billion USD; female: 248.56 billion USD overall).

**(d)** Relationship between GDP per capita (PPP, log-scaled x-axis) and VLW/GDP ratio (y-axis) for 122 LMIC countries. Each bubble represents one country; bubble size is proportional to total DALYs; colour indicates income group. The plot reveals a positive association between national income level and absolute VLW, but also shows that several middle-income countries with high pancreatic cancer burden (e.g., Serbia, Moldova, Ukraine) have VLW/GDP ratios exceeding 1.5%.

### Figure 2. Temporal trends in pancreatic cancer VLW and DALYs across LMICs, 1990–2023.

**(a)** Total VLW for all 122 LMIC countries combined (both sexes), 1990–2023. The solid blue line represents the point estimate; the shaded ribbon denotes the 95% UI. VLW increased from approximately 247 billion USD in 1990 to 646 billion USD in 2023, a 2.6-fold increase over 34 years.

**(b)** VLW trends disaggregated by income group. Coloured lines represent LIC (red), LMIC (orange), and UMIC (teal). Filled circles mark key reference years (1990, 2000, 2010, 2023). UMIC dominates the absolute VLW trajectory, growing from 229 billion to 564 billion USD. LIC shows the steepest relative increase (3.9-fold) despite the smallest absolute values.

**(c)** Total DALYs trends by income group (millions). DALYs increased in parallel across all income strata, rising from 2.32 million (1990) to 6.47 million (2023). The UMIC stratum accounts for the majority of the burden, driven by population ageing and rising incidence in upper middle-income countries such as China and Brazil.

**(d)** Sex-stratified VLW trends (male: blue; female: red). The male–female gap in VLW widened progressively from 1990 to 2023, reflecting the more rapid growth in male pancreatic cancer DALYs, particularly in the 50–74 age group.

### Figure 3. Spatial distribution of pancreatic cancer economic burden across LMICs in 2023.

**(a)** Choropleth map showing total VLW (billion USD) per country, plotted on a log~1+x~ scale using a sequential blue colour gradient. Grey areas indicate non-LMIC or data-unavailable countries. Countries with the highest absolute VLW include China, India, Mexico, Brazil, and Ukraine, reflecting the combined effect of large populations and moderate-to-high age-standardized DALY rates. Legend: graduated blue shading from 0 to 100 billion USD.

**(b)** Choropleth map showing VLW as a percentage of national GDP, plotted on a log~1+x~ scale using a sequential red colour gradient. This metric captures the relative economic welfare impact of pancreatic cancer adjusted for country economic capacity. Highest VLW/GDP ratios are observed in Eastern European countries (Serbia: 2.81%, Moldova: 2.37%, Bosnia and Herzegovina: 1.93%) and select Pacific island nations. Legend: graduated red shading from 0% to 1%.

**(c)** Reference map showing World Bank income classification of the 122 LMIC countries included in this analysis. Red = low income (n = 20); orange = lower middle income (n = 50); teal = upper middle income (n = 52). Grey areas indicate high-income countries or territories not classified as LMIC.

### Figure 4. Age-specific distribution of pancreatic cancer VLW and DALYs in LMICs, 2023.

**(a)** Total VLW by 5-year age group (both sexes, all 122 LMICs). The teal line and ribbon represent the point estimate and 95% UI, respectively. VLW peaks at the 70–74 age group (283.5 billion USD), which is notably older than the DALY peak. This age shift occurs because the value of a statistical life year (VSLY) increases at older ages where remaining healthy life expectancy (HALE) is shorter, thus each DALY commands a higher monetary value.

**(b)** Total DALYs by 5-year age group (both sexes). The purple line and ribbon show point estimates and 95% UI. DALYs peak at the 65–69 age group (1,024,797 DALYs), reflecting the age of highest incidence and mortality. The discrepancy between the DALY peak (65–69) and VLW peak (70–74) has implications for resource allocation: interventions targeting the 70–74 age group may yield the greatest economic welfare return.

**(c)** Sex-stratified VLW by age group. Blue = male; red = female. Males show consistently higher VLW than females from age 40 onward. The male VLW peak occurs at 65–69 years, whereas the female peak occurs at 70–74 years, reflecting differences in both DALY burden and sex-specific HALE.

**(d)** Income group-stratified VLW by age group. Red = LIC; orange = LMIC; teal = UMIC. UMIC countries dominate VLW across all age groups, with the sharpest gradient in the 55–80 age range. LIC countries contribute minimally at all ages owing to lower population size and lower per-capita VSL.

### Figure 5. Projected VLW and DALYs for pancreatic cancer in LMICs, 1990–2050.

Projections are based on exponential smoothing state space models (ETS with damped additive trend) fitted to 1990–2023 observed data. The dashed vertical line marks the boundary between observed data (left) and forecasted values (right). Shaded ribbons denote 95% prediction intervals.

**(a)** Total VLW projection. Blue line and ribbon = observed (1990–2023); red line and ribbon = forecast (2024–2050). VLW is projected to reach 1,016.24 (95% CI: 654.52–1,377.95) billion USD by 2050, representing a 1.57-fold increase from 2023.

**(b)** Total DALYs projection. Purple = observed; orange = forecast. DALYs are projected to increase from 6.47 million (2023) to 10.74 (6.62–14.87) million by 2050. The widening prediction interval reflects growing uncertainty over the 27-year forecast horizon.

**(c)** VLW projections by income group. Each income stratum is forecast independently. UMIC: projected to reach 836.79 (561.06–1,112.52) billion USD by 2050. LMIC: 170.81 (104.65–236.96) billion. LIC: 5.27 (3.86–6.69) billion. Shaded ribbons around each income-group line represent the 95% CI. The LMIC stratum shows the highest proportional growth (2.1-fold from 2023 to 2050).

**(d)** Sex-stratified VLW projections. Blue = male; red = female. Both sexes show continued upward trends; the male–female gap is projected to persist through 2050. Shaded ribbons represent 95% CI for each sex.

### Figure 6. Age-standardized DALY rate trends and VLW fold change, 1990–2023.

**(a)** Mean age-standardized DALY rate (per 100,000 population) for pancreatic cancer across LMIC countries, stratified by income group, 1990–2023. UMIC countries show the highest and most rapidly increasing rates, consistent with population ageing and improved diagnostic ascertainment. LIC countries display the lowest but gradually rising rates, potentially reflecting both true incidence increases and improving cancer registration systems. Colour coding: red = LIC; orange = LMIC; teal = UMIC.

**(b)** Fold change in total VLW between 1990 and 2023, by income group. Bar height represents the ratio of 2023 VLW to 1990 VLW. Labels indicate fold change with "x" suffix. LIC showed the highest relative growth (approximately 3.9x), driven by rapid increases in DALYs from a low baseline. LMIC demonstrated approximately 4.6x growth, while UMIC showed approximately 2.5x growth. Colour coding follows income group convention.

**Source data**: Global Burden of Disease Study 2023 (GBD 2023), Institute for Health Metrics and Evaluation. GDP data from World Bank World Development Indicators (2023). Country income classification from World Bank (2023 fiscal year). VLW calculated using the Viscusi–Masterman value of statistical life transfer method with income elasticity = 1.0 and US VSL reference of $13.2 million (2023). Discount rate for VLW~discounted~ = 3% per annum. Forecasts generated using exponential smoothing state space models (ETS, damped additive trend). All uncertainty intervals from GBD represent 95% UI; all forecast intervals represent 95% CI.
